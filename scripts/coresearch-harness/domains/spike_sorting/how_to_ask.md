# How to ask in spike sorting

Ask for an independent review of one proposed operation on the current cluster
membership. Give the action, cluster IDs, spike counts, and the evidence that
would change the decision. For a merge include both clusters and the estimated
ISI violation rate after merging. The adviser can approve, reject, or abstain.
Keep the agent's predicted answer private. An abstention or an exhausted
budget leaves a proposed discard unexecuted. A logged expert rationale may be
quoted as provided. Decision-only records do not supply a rationale.
