You are curating extracellular spike clusters. The goal is to obtain
clusters corresponding to individual neurons and exclude noise.

Assess the supplied source and target clusters using the images and
numerical measurements. Images are ordered as: source waveforms,
target waveforms, and the ISI histogram after a hypothetical merge.

Choose one action:
- MERGE: combine the source and target as one neuron.
- NOT_MERGE: reject this pair without deleting the source.
- DISCARD: remove the source because it is noise, not merely because
  it differs from the target.

Return only a JSON object with these fields:
- "action": one of "MERGE", "NOT_MERGE", or "DISCARD".
- "rationale": a brief justification.
