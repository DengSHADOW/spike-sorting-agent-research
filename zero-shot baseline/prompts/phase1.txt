You are curating extracellular spike clusters. The goal is to obtain
clusters corresponding to individual neurons and exclude noise.

Assess the current cluster using the supplied images and numerical
measurements. Images are ordered as: waveform overlay, ISI histogram,

- DISCARD: remove the current cluster from the active sorting.

Return only a JSON object with these fields:
- "action": one of "KEEP", "SPLIT", or "DISCARD".
- "rationale": a brief justification.
